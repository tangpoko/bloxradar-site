# How small and mid-size Roblox developers launch and grow games (2025-2026)

Research date: 28 October 2026. About 15 searches and fetches. Primary Roblox sources (DevForum announcements, Roblox newsroom) are used wherever possible. Everything else is a trade outlet, a vendor blog or a fan site, and each one is labelled. Items older than 2025 are marked.

## 1. Launch tactics with evidence

### Takeaway
The best documented tactic is a fixed weekly update day with a scheduled live event built around it. Grow a Garden and Steal a Brainrot both used this, and both grew around it. Thumbnail personalization (up to five rotating thumbnails) is the only optimisation tool from Roblox with a published lift figure. I found almost no hard evidence on launch weekday, hour, timezone or school-holiday timing. Most "launch playbooks" online are vendor blogs.

### Cited Findings
**Weekly updates and scheduled events**
- When Jandel's studio, Splitting Point, partnered with Grow a Garden, the game had about 500 CCU and 10,000 to 20,000 DAU. His first change was "We started weekly updates." He reports about 100,000 CCU within roughly two weeks and 1 million CCU within about a month. Splitting Point is known for weekly updates across all its games. (GamesBeat interview, 7 Sep 2025) — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/janzen-madsen-interview/)
- Steal a Brainrot holds a weekly Saturday content drop with an "admin abuse" event around 3:00 PM ET, plus a Tuesday "Taco Tuesday" event at 6:00 PM ET lasting about 30 to 45 minutes. This comes from a fan and marketplace blog, so it is secondary. — [Eldorado](https://www.eldorado.gg/blog/steal-a-brainrot-admin-abuse-schedule-explained/)
- Jandel ran Grow a Garden "admin weather events" before each weekly update. Steal a Brainrot's developer copied the format. A staged "admin abuse war" between the two games on 23 Aug 2025 is credited with pushing Steal a Brainrot to a 20M peak and Roblox to a platform record of 47.4M concurrent users. — [Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot); [Beebom](https://beebom.com/poll-who-will-win-admin-abuse-war-grow-a-garden-vs-steal-a-brainrot/); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/robloxs-peak-concurrent-user-count-hits-record-474m-as-steal-a-brainrot-and-grow-a-garden-compete/)
- Steal a Brainrot's single-day "Extinct Event" on 13 Sep 2025 pushed its peak to 24M, while its monthly average was about 1M CCU. A Bruno Mars concert in January 2026 peaked at 12.8M. This shows how far one event day can spike above the normal baseline. — [Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- A single dev write-up describes one game going from 1,000 to 3,000 CCU during an update-day event. It recommends teasing updates with sneak peeks and blurred screenshots. This is anecdotal. — [daily.dev](https://daily.dev/posts/how-i-made-a-viral-roblox-game-wckqwbgbr)
- One blog opinion: Grow a Garden's weekly updates later became repetitive and Jandel moved his attention to a new game. — [TMS Press](https://tmspress.org/9409/fun-zone/grow-a-garden-what-happened-to-it/)

**Thumbnails and icons**
- Thumbnail personalization: creators can set up to five active thumbnails, and it switches on once two or more are active. Impressions start evenly split. Every hour, thumbnails with better play-through for specific user groups get more impressions. It is a multi-armed bandit, not an A/B test. Roblox reported an average 8.5% lift in qPTR, with some games seeing 50%. About ten developers had tested it at the time. (Article from Nov 2024, updated June 2025.) — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/roblox-will-let-game-devs-personalize-thumbnails-to-attract-more-players/)
- The same announcement introduced the "Our Charts" page with trending and up-and-coming games by genre, and editorial "Today's Picks" on Home. — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/roblox-will-let-game-devs-personalize-thumbnails-to-attract-more-players/)
- Since June 2026, Roblox says it will test thumbnail personalization on the new PTR metric instead of qPTR. — [DevForum, 15 Jun 2026](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575)
- Icon testing does not exist. Icon A/B testing is an open DevForum feature request, so developers infer icon performance indirectly. (Vendor blog.) — [CreatorXP](https://creatorxp.gg/guides/roblox-game-icon-mistakes). A developer asked on the DevForum where to see icon performance because they suspected their icon caused a qPTR drop. The reply was that icon impressions can only be estimated from Continue Play returning-user data. — [DevForum, Mar 2025](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441)

**Soft launch, Discord, testing groups**
- A vendor "8-week playbook" recommends inviting a Discord community into a closed beta and fixing the icon and thumbnail set in the final weeks before launch. This is vendor advice from a marketing agency, not measured evidence. — [BLOXG](https://bloxg.com/guides/roblox-game-launch-strategy)
- Developers also use reserved servers (private servers) for testing groups. Since 11 Dec 2025, play in reserved servers counts as "intentional co-play" for the algorithm. — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441)

**Seasonality and timing**
- Roblox's own list of things that move Home impressions includes "total users and seasonality (weekly, summer, holiday)", alongside your own updates, algorithm changes, and competing games doing better. — [DevForum, 15 Jun 2026](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575)
- Signal changes update within about 24 hours, but show up in impressions a few days later. Roblox's example was 28 Feb to 3 Mar. — [DevForum, Mar 2025](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441)

**Trend chasing and how fast trends move**
- Dubit (a large Roblox studio) says the biggest new games ride trend waves: internet memes such as "brainrot", genre surges (escape, wave survival, obby, tycoon) and "hype" from twists or social chaos. Its advice is "evolution beats revolution" and to win the player over in about 30 seconds. (Sep 2026) — [Dubit](https://dubit.io/library/what-makes-a-new-roblox-game-take-off)
- Speed example: Grow a Garden was built in about three days by a 16-year-old (BMWLux) and launched 26 Mar 2025. — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/janzen-madsen-interview/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden). Steal a Brainrot launched 16 May 2025 on the 2025 "Italian brainrot" meme and averaged about 356K CCU by June. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot); [Max Power Gaming](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/from-gardens-to-brainrot-the-most-popular-roblox-games-of-q2-2025)
- A third-party tracker showed a whole "Brainrot" genre at 1.4M CCU across 29 games in Feb 2026. It also showed a spin-off, "Escape Tsunami For Brainrots", down 78.1% in CCU in one week. This suggests copycat waves can collapse within weeks. The tool is self-reported. — [DevForum, RoLearn "Market Friday", 20 Feb 2026](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/market-friday-an-ml-tool-that-tracks-roblox-genre-trends-%E2%80%94-heres-this-weeks-full-market-report/4413816)
- Clones are pushed onto Roblox quickly to take advantage of hype. Platforms mostly require AI-usage labels rather than blocking copies. (Oct 2026) — [Gamemeca](https://www.gamemeca.com/en/view.php?gid=1782071)

**TikTok, YouTube and creators**
- Steal a Brainrot spread through TikTok and YouTube. Strategy videos and clips of upset children whose Brainrots were stolen got tens of millions of views. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- Jandel has about 12M TikTok followers. The developer became a personality in his own right. — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/janzen-madsen-interview/)

### Inferences
- An update day leaves a visible mark in public data: a burst of edits to the place, often new badges or game passes, and a CCU spike at a fixed hour. A site that logs these changes over time can show competitors' update days and hours, and how big each spike was. Today developers get this from fan blogs.
- Because thumbnail personalization rotates up to five images, the thumbnails a game shows publicly change over time. Logging them could show which thumbnail sets top games use. This is a guess: I did not check whether the public thumbnails API returns all active personalized thumbnails or only a default.
- Trend waves seem to last weeks, not months, for copycats. That makes "what is rising this week in genre X" more useful than monthly rankings.

### Gaps
- No reliable data found on the best launch weekday, hour, timezone or school-holiday effect. Saturday is the common update day for top games (Grow a Garden and Steal a Brainrot), but I found no measurement comparing days.
- No r/robloxgamedev threads were surfaced by search, so grassroots launch advice (group shouts, codes, cross-promotion between a studio's own games) is not covered with sources here.
- No sourced data on how much group shouts, codes or cross-promotion contribute.

## 2. Metrics that decide whether the algorithm picks up a game

### Takeaway
Roblox publishes the signals. Since 15 June 2026, Recommended For You looks at 28 days of retention (day 1, days 2 to 7, days 8 to 28) plus play-through rate and first-play bounce. qPTR was removed. Only players who joined from Recommended For You count. Roblox has never published a concurrent-player threshold, and new developers openly asked how new games get evaluated under a 28-day window. They got no answer.

### Cited Findings
- **Before June 2026 (from 31 Mar 2025):** the signals were qPTR (engaging plays divided by Recommended For You impressions), 7-day playtime per user (capped at 60 minutes per user, per game, per day), 7-day play days, 7-day spend days, 7-day Robux spent, and 7-day intentional co-play days. More than 90% of traffic begins on Home. Signals are averaged per user "so smaller experiences are not disadvantaged". A FAQ added in Dec 2025 says no single signal is most important. — [DevForum, 31 Mar 2025, updated 11 Dec 2025](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441)
- **From 15 June 2026:** the window went from 7 to 28 days, split into day 1, days 2 to 7 and days 8 to 28. Signals kept: playtime, play days, qualified play sessions, intentional co-play days, spend days, Robux spent. qPTR was removed. PTR and first-play bounce rates were added. Only users who "organically joined from the Recommended for You sort on Home" count. Benchmarks do not affect the algorithm. — [DevForum, 15 Jun 2026](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575)
- **Weighting, as reported by Dubit:** four "Most Important" signals are play-through rate, first-play bounce rate (measured in two windows, under 60 seconds and 61 to 180 seconds), play days per user and playtime per user. Four "Important" signals are intentional co-play days, qualified play sessions, spend days and Robux spent. Roblox published the weighting only as a chart image. — [Dubit](https://dubit.io/library/what-makes-a-new-roblox-game-take-off)
- Roblox's stated reason: overvaluing short-term engagement favoured games with eye-catching thumbnails and little lasting value. Those games displaced games players valued long term. The new algorithm raised DAU and engagement in testing, but no figures were given. Roblox had 132M DAU in Q1 2026. — [Roblox newsroom, John Ciancutti, 15 Jun 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox)
- **Developer concerns in the thread:**
  - Three developers asked how new games are evaluated if the window is 28 days. Before, it was about one week. One asked whether a cold-start system exists. There was no answer in the thread.
  - A developer warned that players on 1.5GB RAM devices who crash early would count as bounces.
  - Others questioned excluding search, ad and friend-join traffic.
  - — [DevForum, 15 Jun 2026](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575)
- AI-made games are ranked by player retention like any other game. Roblox says the homepage bar is unchanged. — [TechCrunch, 16 Jul 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/16/roblox-launches-an-ai-powered-game-creation-feature-in-its-mobile-app/); [Central Jersey](https://centraljersey.com/2026/07/20/roblox-build-ai-text-to-game/)

### Inferences
- These signals are private and per game. A third-party site cannot see them for other games. Public proxies are CCU curves, visits growth, favourites and likes ratios, and how fast CCU comes back after a dip. Those can show whether a competitor is being pushed by Recommended For You. This is a guess about how useful the proxies are, not something tested.
- With a 28-day window, a new game probably needs a month of stable retention before full Home exposure. If so, early CCU growth over days 1 to 30 is a meaningful public chart for developers comparing launches. This is a guess. Roblox has not confirmed how cold start works.

### Gaps
- No official concurrent-player threshold or minimum-traffic threshold. I found none published.
- No answer from Roblox on cold start for new games under the 28-day model.
- Exact signal weights exist only as an image in the DevForum post. I could not read it.

## 3. What developers want to see about other games, and the tools they use

### Takeaway
Developers already use a crowded set of mostly free or cheap third-party trackers: RoMonitor, Rolimon's, RoLearn, CCUCheck, GGAID, RTrack, Dive, and the shut-down Rotrends. These cover CCU and genre trends. The 2026 comparison I found does not list game-pass price tracking, thumbnail or icon change tracking, update-day detection or trend alerts for any of them. Inside Roblox, developers asked for icon performance data and co-play graphs that do not exist.

### Cited Findings
- **Tool comparison (Aug 2026, vendor blog):** — [lensblox](https://lensblox.com/blog/best-roblox-game-analytics-tools-compared-2026-guide/)
  - RoMonitor Stats: free, ad-supported browser extension overlaying stats on Roblox pages. Mostly used by casual players.
  - Rolimon's Game Analytics: free stats and graphs inside a trading site.
  - RoLearn: free, $19 and $49 tiers. Trends, forecasts, AI game ideas, revenue simulation. Criticised on the DevForum as "vibe-coded".
  - CCUCheck: free, $29 Pro, and $299 to $2,000+ datasets aimed at investors.
  - GGAID: charts for 26,000+ games.
  - RTrack: live CCU leaderboard refreshed about every 30 seconds.
  - Dive: enterprise, custom quote.
  - BLOXG: a marketing agency with analytics on the side.
  - Rotrends: discontinued.
  - The article says none of these track search keyword ranking. It does not mention game-pass, thumbnail or alert features for any tool.
- RoLearn's metrics: "Opportunity Score" from CCU demand, saturation, competition density and growth. Also genre totals, average CCU per game, 7 and 14-day CCU forecasts using Prophet. Its own example report had internal inconsistencies in game counts. The single reply was cautious ("seems too good to be true"). — [DevForum, Feb 2026](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/market-friday-an-ml-tool-that-tracks-roblox-genre-trends-%E2%80%94-heres-this-weeks-full-market-report/4413816)
- Rotrends was announced on the DevForum as a trends, keywords and game-stats tool. It is now listed as shut down. — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rotrends-%E2%80%93-analyze-trends-keywords-and-game-stats/3471292); [lensblox](https://lensblox.com/blog/best-roblox-game-analytics-tools-compared-2026-guide/)
- Chrome extensions also exist ("Roblox Game Analyzer", "Roblox Game Analytics & Competitor Tracker"). — [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/roblox-game-analytics-com/pmlaldpjilkkngekicgcbjeipklmfgnj)
- GameAnalytics publishes an annual Roblox benchmark report. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-roblox-analytics-stack-how-studios-win-with-data-in-2026)
- **Requests on Roblox's own forum:**
  - Where to see icon performance.
  - A co-play percentage graph.
  - Why a game's "similar experiences" list compares an obby with unrelated games.
  - Genre filters and search.
  - — [DevForum, Mar 2025](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441); [DevForum, Jun 2026](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575)
- Revenue estimates exist on third-party sites but are unverified. For example, 99 Nights in the Forest is estimated at about $15.18M a year. — [profitable.app](https://profitable.app/roblox/games/99-nights-in-the-forest)

### Inferences
- The market already has plenty of CCU leaderboards. The less covered areas are change history: when a game updated, what it added (badges, passes, price changes), when its thumbnail or icon changed, and what the CCU did over the next 24 to 72 hours. That matches BloxRadar's change logs, badges and game pass data. This is an inference from the gaps the comparison lists, not from a developer survey.
- Game-pass price benchmarks by genre ("what do top tycoons charge for 2x money") can be built from public game-pass data. I found no tool advertising this.
- Trust is a problem. Developers are sceptical of "AI-scored" trend tools with inconsistent numbers. Clear raw data with honest sampling notes may count for more than predictions.

### Gaps
- No survey of what developers want from competitor tools. The evidence is scattered forum requests and vendor blogs.
- Market share and paid usage of these tools are unknown.
- Bloxbiz (Super Biz) and other ad or analytics vendors did not appear in results.

## 4. Case studies of games that broke out without big ad spend

### Takeaway
The 2025 mega-hits started small and fast. A simple core loop was built in days, and often a meme or a social mechanic was the hook. An experienced live-ops studio then joined early (at about 500 CCU for Grow a Garden) and added weekly updates and scheduled events. Short-form video did most of the marketing. I found no developer-sourced breakout account for Dead Rails, Fisch or Dress to Impress.

### Cited Findings
- **Grow a Garden:**
  - Built in about three days by a 16-year-old, BMWLux, and launched 26 Mar 2025.
  - Jandel's Splitting Point bought in at about 500 CCU (other sources say about 1,000) and started weekly updates. The game reached about 100K CCU in two weeks and 1M in a month. Do Big Studios later took a minority stake.
  - Jandel credits the broad farming theme and plants that keep growing while you are offline. That was new on Roblox.
  - Peak 22.3M CCU, about 60M peak DAU and about 30B plays by Sep 2025.
  - — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/janzen-madsen-interview/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
  - Another source puts the growth at 142K CCU in April and 2.5M in June 2025. — [Max Power Gaming](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/from-gardens-to-brainrot-the-most-popular-roblox-games-of-q2-2025)
  - Guinness record of 21.6M concurrent in July 2025. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- **Steal a Brainrot:**
  - By SpyderSammy, owned by DoBig Studios. Launched 16 May 2025.
  - The hook is the "Italian brainrot" meme, plus a steal-from-other-players loop compared to capture the flag.
  - Spread through TikTok and YouTube clips of stolen Brainrots and upset kids.
  - Peaks: 5M in July 2025, 20M on 23 Aug 2025 (admin war), 24M on 13 Sep 2025 (Extinct Event), 25.4M in Oct 2025.
  - Later licensed as a Fortnite island and for a film.
  - Criticised for pay-to-win mechanics and cheaters.
  - — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/robloxs-steal-a-brainrot-becomes-first-game-to-surpass-25m-concurrent-players/)
- **99 Nights in the Forest:**
  - By Grandma's Favourite Games, three New Zealand creators who formed a group in 2020. Launched March 2025 in the co-op survival horror genre.
  - About 26B visits in a little over a year and a 14.2M all-time peak.
  - Theatrical rights were sold to 20th Century Studios.
  - Jandel cites it as another example of how common hits are on Roblox.
  - — [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/movies/20th-century-studios-roblox-game-99-nights-in-the-forest/); [AV Club](https://www.avclub.com/roblox-99-nights-in-the-forest-movie); [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/janzen-madsen-interview/)
  - In Feb 2026 it was at 615K CCU on a third-party tracker. — [DevForum RoLearn](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/market-friday-an-ml-tool-that-tracks-roblox-genre-trends-%E2%80%94-heres-this-weeks-full-market-report/4413816)
- **Dead Rails:** RCM Games. Ranked #3 in Q2 2025 player averages. TikTok shows it paired with 99 Nights as a rival, and later nostalgia posts suggest its peak has passed. No breakout account found. — [Fandomwire](https://fandomwire.com/scariest-roblox-horror-games-halloween/); [Max Power Gaming](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/from-gardens-to-brainrot-the-most-popular-roblox-games-of-q2-2025)
- **Fish It** (a 2025-2026 hit): 472.5K CCU in Feb 2026, per a third-party tracker. — [DevForum RoLearn](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/market-friday-an-ml-tool-that-tracks-roblox-genre-trends-%E2%80%94-heres-this-weeks-full-market-report/4413816)
- **Platform context:**
  - Roblox reported $4.9B revenue for FY2025, up 36%. — [games.gg](https://games.gg/news/roblox-revenue-hits-4-billion-in-2025-boosted-by-grow-a-garden/)
  - More than 25 experiences have over 1M DAU (Nov 2024). — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/roblox-will-let-game-devs-personalize-thumbnails-to-attract-more-players/)
  - Since 2025, Recommended For You can drive hundreds of millions of plays to one game. This is Dubit's view. — [Dubit](https://dubit.io/library/what-makes-a-new-roblox-game-take-off)

### Inferences
- A repeated pattern is that an experienced studio spots a small game early and buys in. Jandel did this at about 500 to 1,000 CCU. Studios like Splitting Point and DoBig are scouting for rising small games, which makes "games with the fastest CCU growth from a low base" a real audience need for scouts, not only for developers.
- Event spikes of 10 to 20 times the monthly average CCU (Steal a Brainrot: about 1M average against a 24M event peak) mean a chart needs fine time resolution to show them. Daily averages would hide them.

### Gaps
- No developer-sourced breakout story found for Dead Rails, Fisch or Dress to Impress. Dress to Impress is from 2024, so it is older and outside the focus anyway.
- No named 2026 small-game breakouts with developer accounts. Fish It and Grow a Garden 2 (12 Jun 2026, single source, unverified) appear only in tracker data. — [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/did-jandel-make-grow-a-garden-2-the-developer-story-explained/)
- No data on how much (if anything) these games spent on ads early on.

## 5. The AI-generated game flood and how quality developers stand out

### Takeaway
Roblox launched "Build", an AI tool that makes a game from a text prompt in the mobile app, in July 2026. Roblox says its answer to the flood is ranking by retention. The June 2026 algorithm change, which penalises "quick click" games with nice thumbnails but low retention, is the main way quality games get separated. There is no published count of how many AI games exist.

### Cited Findings
- Roblox launched Build, prompt-to-game in the mobile app, in July 2026. Critics worry about repetitive low-quality games. Roblox says AI games are ranked by retention like any other, so unplayed games sink. A GDC survey found 52% of industry professionals think generative AI is having a negative impact. — [TechCrunch, 16 Jul 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/16/roblox-launches-an-ai-powered-game-creation-feature-in-its-mobile-app/); [VOI](https://voi.id/en/technology/585316)
- Developers report fast AI-assisted copying of games to take advantage of hype. Platforms mainly rely on AI-usage labels. (Oct 2026) — [Gamemeca](https://www.gamemeca.com/en/view.php?gid=1782071)
- One player anecdote says AI-made Roblox games are hard to tell apart and sometimes get more players. This is a single YouTube community post. — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/post/Ugkx8vkKxnmGf5LDWevM6jhQE0Y2pq6fdv2Q)
- Roblox explicitly moved discovery away from games that "win a quick click but don't offer deeper substance". It moved toward 28-day retention, co-play and spend. — [Roblox newsroom, Jun 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox)
- The ways quality games stand out, as practised by hit studios: live ops (weekly updates and scheduled events), social mechanics that make players bring friends (co-play is a ranked signal), and a fast first 30 seconds (first-play bounce under 60 seconds is a top signal). — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/janzen-madsen-interview/); [Dubit](https://dubit.io/library/what-makes-a-new-roblox-game-take-off)

### Inferences
- As AI makes building games cheaper, the scarce part becomes live operations and knowing the market. A site that shows who updates on a schedule and how players respond helps the developers who compete on live ops.
- A public "new games that are holding players" view (CCU still present on day 7, 14 and 28 after the first appearance) would roughly copy Roblox's own 28-day logic with public data. That would help sort the flood. This is a guess that it would work well. It needs testing against real data.

### Gaps
- No numbers on how many games are created per day, or what share is AI-made. The "thousands a day" figure in the brief was not checked here.
- No data on retention of AI-generated games compared with hand-made ones.
